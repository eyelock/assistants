A claim's lease runs out five minutes after the claim and bd's lease length is not configurable, so seats refresh it with `bd heartbeat <id>` at least every few minutes. A lapsed lease alone frees nothing: the bead stays in progress and assigned, and another seat's claim is still refused, until the dispatcher reclaims it with `bd reclaim`, once it has confirmed the seat is gone, after which the old holder's heartbeat is refused and it must stop. Seats never reclaim or `--force` another seat's bead.

- [#108](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/108) [`b94b4e4`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/b94b4e478b0cf71b9295bf5ad6803bcd8341ccca) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - A build bead closes when its pull request merges, never at review. A fix a review asks for is a bead for the pull request's author, and the reviewer closes its own review bead when the verdict is posted. The `work-graph` skill's closing section and the starter-team worker, specialist worker and reviewer templates now say so.

- [#116](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/116) [`2f94a54`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/2f94a5422de90f3effc07da4ad1dc4f72e892c2e) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - The `comms` skill asks the human one question at a time instead of a batch, and reports lead with the initiative, in plain words, and stay small, after a dense report was found confusing.

- [#88](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/88) [`516914c`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/516914c622ce4621f6dc2cebd11350aa1c9862bb) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - The docs catch up with the humans and the inbox. The plugin README's layers and skills tables name the humans, the default human and the inbox, and what `work-graph`, `wake` and `doctor` do about them; the Wheelhouse overview says who the humans are and how a seat asks one for something, with the `repo:` label from `--repo` or the bead it blocks. The `wake` skill's fallback, when there is no wake script, has the desk run `inbox.sh show` too. `inbox.sh`'s usage for `show` lists the `priority` field it prints.

- [#134](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/134) [`61879fb`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/61879fbe176b59f31c0568a6b8020b9fdcc140ac) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - The worker and specialist worker focus prompts and May lines, and the add-seat standing order, now cover both handovers: a bead handed over as a pull request stays in progress until it merges, and one handed over any other way (uncommitted changes, the default) closes with what happened.

- [#136](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/136) [`9de3af9`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/9de3af9b9823249f7488dd68ba4b17ca4b63fb84) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - Who is idle: a worker whose only in-progress beads have open pull requests is free for new work, and the dispatcher's May-not and Dispatching step 7 agree it may be given a new bead. `open-pr.sh` notes each pull request on its bead as `PR: <url>`, the one signal the idle check, the wake and the never-a-dead-claim rule read; the wake marks an in-progress bead "(waiting on PR <url>)" when its pull request is open. A bead with no pull request closes when its worker has handed the work over. Adds a dispatch eval, and tests for the note and the wake mark.

- [#88](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/88) [`516914c`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/516914c622ce4621f6dc2cebd11350aa1c9862bb) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - `inbox.sh` handles odd titles and names. `assign` passes the title with `--title`, so a title starting with `-` is created rather than read as a flag, and, without `--kind`, takes a title with the word "merge" or "review" anywhere in it (such as "Merge: widget [#13](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/issues/13)" or "Please merge widget [#13](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/issues/13)") as a merge or review that needs `--url`. `show --name` makes the human's command from the name in lowercase ASCII (`[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]*`), and refuses a name with no letter or digit in it.

- [#88](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/88) [`516914c`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/516914c622ce4621f6dc2cebd11350aa1c9862bb) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - Inbox beads name their repository, as every task does. `inbox.sh assign` takes `--repo <repo>` and labels the bead `repo:<repo>`; without it, the bead takes the `repo:` label of the bead it blocks. A merge or review is refused without a repository; a decision or credential that touches none goes unlabelled, and the work-graph skill says so.

- [#118](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/pull/118) [`c2660cb`](https://github.com/eyelock/assistants/commit/c2660cbcc2e33864802d93c16c7c2ca11c27725f) Thanks [@eyelock](https://github.com/eyelock)! - The wake skill gives a path that resolves for a seat the desk wakes as a subagent: the Wheelhouse's own `scripts/wake.sh`, run from its root, or by its full path with `--dir <wheelhouse>` from anywhere, with the exact `--seat <command>` form. It no longer says `path/to/wake/scripts/wake.sh`. `wake.sh` takes the seat's name bare as well (`wake.sh hynde`), refuses two different seats or two bare names with usage, and, when it can't tell which seat it is, stops with usage and the roster's seats. The seat is not guessed from the harness: nothing a running seat can read names it reliably.
