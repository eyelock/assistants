`inbox.sh` gains `close` (alias `done`): it closes a human's inbox bead with a note, as the seat recording it (`inbox.sh close <bead> --reason <text>`). Only the desk runs it: it refuses any other seat. It refuses a bead with no `inbox` label or one assigned to a seat, so the `--force` it needs cannot close a seat's work. The work-graph skill documents it.

The work-graph skill and the constitution that create-wheelhouse writes name it as the one forced close a seat may make of a bead it does not hold.
