Specialist workers: a worker's roster seat can list `specialities`, bead labels such as `repo:ynh`, and takes only beads carrying one. The dispatcher gives a bead to an idle specialist first, falls through to a general-purpose worker when the specialists are busy, and falls back to one when nobody has the speciality. add-seat records specialities and offers a general-purpose worker when there is none; the doctor warns when every worker is a specialist.
