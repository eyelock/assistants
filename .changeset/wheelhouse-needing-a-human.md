The work-graph skill gains "Needing a human": when work needs a human (a merge, a decision, a credential), a seat assigns a bead with `scripts/inbox.sh assign`, blocking the work it holds up and saying who asked and what done means, to a named person only when it knows who and otherwise to the default human; it tells the humans things that need no action with `scripts/inbox.sh note`, naming anything on the web by its short form as a Markdown link.
