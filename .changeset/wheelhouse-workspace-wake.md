Seats never wake bare, and ask for the folders they work in (ynh 0.15.0 or later). add-seat and create-wheelhouse give every seat, the desk included, a `wake` focus as its `launch_focus`, so a seat started by its name acts on its wake block straight away, while `--focus <order>` still runs one standing order. Each seat's `workspace` asks for only the repositories its job touches, from the constitution's new `## What it reaches`. A request grants nothing: the human grants it with `ynh trust accept` or their own `workspace_roots`, and the skills never do either for them. The doctor's new checks warn about a seat with no launch focus, and about workspace folders that don't exist.
