The `add-seat` and `create-wheelhouse` skills state the rule for a seat's group plainly: a seat is crew when the human works with it directly (a designer, a reviewer, a tutor, a programme architect) and standing when it runs on the desk's or a timer's say-so (a dispatcher, a CI watcher, a releaser). `add-seat` asks that one question to choose the group, and `create-wheelhouse` carries it into the naming-pool question and the roster's naming rules.
