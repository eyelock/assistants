A `vet-skill` skill: how a seat vets and pins a skill someone else wrote before including it. It covers the four conditions of a vetted skill (a named publisher and clear license, every file read, a SkillSpector scan of the folder as it is included with 0 active findings, a fit with the seat's job), pinning by full commit SHA, the `pinned-skills.md` record, and the one-finding exception rule: one finding, one exact SHA, accepted by a human in their own message, with the reason recorded, never granted by a worker. It offers the SkillSpector CI job as an opt-in, not a default.
