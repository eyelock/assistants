How finished work is handed over is the Wheelhouse's choice: create-wheelhouse asks, and writes a `## Handing over work` section into the constitution (uncommitted for review, or a draft pull request; a human merges either way), and add-seat writes the worker charter to match. A crew seat raises what is outside its mandate with the human it works with; standing roles and workers hand it back to the desk. Every desk includes ynh's own `ynh-compose` and `ynh-troubleshoot` skills, to add skills and features to any seat.
