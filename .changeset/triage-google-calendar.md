The `google-calendar` harness answers more triage questions. It answers `catch-up` (an event's organizer, time, place and who has answered) and `find` (when and where a meeting is, and how to join); `my-commitments` is listed as not applicable, because a calendar holds meetings, not promises written in words. The `google-calendar` skill says where to look for each.
