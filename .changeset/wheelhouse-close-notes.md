Every seat writes a two-line close note when it closes or hands over a bead: one ynm memory in its own namespace `seat/<command>`, type reflective, tag `close-note`, subject the bead id. The first line says what surprised it, the second where the sharp edges were. The `work-graph` skill's Closing section gains the step, and the worker, specialist worker, reviewer, dispatcher and verifier templates say the same. The wake block gains "Your close notes", the seat's latest three, and its JSON a `closeNotes` list; with none, nothing is shown.
