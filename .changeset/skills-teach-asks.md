The `work-graph` skill teaches asks between seats: the four kinds (`ask:question`, `ask:request`, `ask:return`, `ask:report`) and who closes each, addressing an ask to the seat that answers it (to `ynw` only when the asker doesn't know who does), and the check-in rule, `ask.sh show` at each natural break. The `comms` skill teaches how to reach a seat (its live session is `<wheelhouse>-<command>`, the desk just `<wheelhouse>`), to nudge the answering seat where the vendor can message a session and comment on the ask when it couldn't, and never to ask the human which session is which. A new work-graph eval shows a seat opening an ask for another seat instead of messaging the human.
