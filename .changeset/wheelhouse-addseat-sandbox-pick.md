`add-seat` gains a throwaway-sandbox exception to the trial: a Wheelhouse with no pull requests, named as a throwaway sandbox by the human, has its seats written straight onto the working branch with their roster entries and delegates, still one at a time with install, trust and birth. The README, `create-wheelhouse` and the tutorial's skip notes cite it, so no skill or doc states the trial as having no exception. `add-seat`'s harness example also gives a seat other than the desk a `pick` of every wheelhouse skill but `refresh`, which stays the desk's alone (the desk includes the whole plugin); a skill the plugin gains later joins a seat's `pick`.
