The starter team offers an optional programme manager, a standing role after the releaser. It runs the Wheelhouse's roadmap on its issue tracker, so real issues close against versioned milestones; makes routine milestone changes itself and reviews bigger ones with the humans first; keeps a roadmap record in a ynm namespace only it writes; and reports weekly, and when a milestone moves, as report items in the humans' inbox. It has two focuses, `sweep` and `report`, and never writes code, merges, releases, designs or dispatches. A starter-team template can now list more than one focus. The add-seat skill, the plugin README, the Wheelhouse overview and the tutorial mention it, and a new eval checks the team walk offers it as optional.
