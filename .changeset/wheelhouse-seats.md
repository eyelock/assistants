Seats are born and wake as themselves: a `birth` skill for a seat's first session (its own identity, pinned in its own namespace; a birth meets the team and takes no work) and a `wake` skill and script that start every session with purpose, the briefing carrying the command to retire handoff notes once picked up. work-graph gains dispatching, shared out fairly to the workers who have built least, and assigned-first pickup; add-seat seats every worker as a seat of its own, started by its own name, commits each seat and refreshes the desk, leaves the births of seats the human works with to the human, and wakes workers with the flags it was given. Every seat names itself in the work graph (`--actor`) rather than trusting `BEADS_ACTOR`; the doctor checks each seat is wired that way. Workers leave their changes uncommitted for review. handoff keeps its notes as episodic memory in the seat's namespace, since ynm drops working memory when a session ends.
