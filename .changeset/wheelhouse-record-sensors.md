Eight record sensors under `sensors/record/`, declared in the plugin's manifest as advisory `command` sensors: `stale-claims` (an in-progress bead with no live claim), `merged-not-closed` (an open bead whose `PR:` has merged), `ready-without-ask` (a pull request out of draft with a ready verdict and no merge bead), `ask-for-merged` (an open merge bead whose pull request has merged or closed), `finished-uncut` (a milestone with nothing open and no release tag), `scope-after-open` (an issue added to an open milestone on a later day than it was created), `patrol-alive` (the patrol's last run is over twice its interval old) and `guard-log` (guard log lines since the last patrol). Each prints one line per item and runs without ynh. They carry no ratchet: `sensors/run-record-sensors.sh`, which the patrol runs on every patrol that saves its snapshot, keeps each count in `.wheelhouse/record-sensors.json` and reports a count that rose as a `record-sensor-rose` change. The desk's wake prints one line per sensor above zero, one for a sensor that could not run, and a warning when the results are older than twice the patrol's interval. `patrol.sh` gains `--interval` and `--no-sensors`, its snapshot records `at` and `interval_minutes`, and the repository discovery moved into `wheelhouse-lib.sh` as `wl_repos`.
