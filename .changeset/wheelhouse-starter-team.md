A starter team: add-seat walks the human through a Wheelhouse's first seats role by role (designer, reviewer, dispatcher, CI watcher, releaser, a general-purpose worker and optional specialists), suggesting each name from its pool and asking only what the role needs. Each role is an editable Markdown template in `add-seat/assets/starter-team/`; a Wheelhouse can replace one or add its own in `skills/roster/assets/starter-team/`. create-wheelhouse offers the walk when it is done, and the desk runs it when asked to set up the team.
