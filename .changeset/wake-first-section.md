The desk's wake block gains a "Wake first" section right after the inbox, naming the roster's seats with the role `ci-watcher` (for the open pull requests and CI) and `releaser` (for the release in hand), each to be woken on its roster model or messaged if live, with both reports relayed beside the inbox. It is `wakeFirst` in the JSON (`[]` for a desk with neither role, `null` for every other seat), and the wake skill describes it as step 0a.
