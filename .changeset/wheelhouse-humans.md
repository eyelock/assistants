Every Wheelhouse has humans. The roster gains a `humans` list beside `seats`, and create-wheelhouse starts it with one default human, named at setup from the Wheelhouse's themes: the name for work that needs a human but no particular one. The constitution gains a rule to ask a human through the inbox (assign a bead, the default human unless you know who; tell them things with an inbox note; never wait silently), and a person can name themselves at the desk ("it's me, <name>") to see their own inbox for that session, with no roster entry.
