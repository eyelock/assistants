The humans' inbox script, `work-graph/scripts/inbox.sh`, copied into every Wheelhouse as `scripts/inbox.sh`. `assign` makes a bead for a human (the default human unless `--to` names one), created as the seat that asked, with `--url` kept as its external reference and `--blocks` holding up the bead it waits for; a merge or review without a URL is refused. `note` leaves an FYI in ynm, namespace `human/<command>`, tagged `inbox`. `register` adds a person who names themselves at the desk, with their git email. `show` prints, as JSON, the ready beads and notes for the human at the desk (matched by git email, or `WHEELHOUSE_HUMAN_EMAIL`) and for the default human, each bead with who asked, its short form and URL, and how many beads it unblocks. Every name comes from the roster. If bd or ynm fails, `show` says so rather than showing an empty inbox: it exits 3, lists what failed in `errors`, sets that human's `beads` or `notes` to null and `empty` to null, and still prints what the other tool returned.
