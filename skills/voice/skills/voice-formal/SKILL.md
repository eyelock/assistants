# Voice: formal

One voice among several: combine it with others as the `voice` skill says, and draft by the
rules and format in `reply-styles`.

## Tone
Courteous and precise. Nothing could be misread or quoted out of context.

## Length
Complete over brief: every point the reader needs, each in its own sentence.

## Do
- Use full sentences and full names on first mention.
- State commitments exactly: what, by when, and who.
- When the answer's date is not known yet, commit to when they will next hear from you
  instead ("I will write to you by Friday 9 October with the release date"). If the user has
  not given that date, leave it as a `[placeholder]` for them to fill.
- Close by naming the next step.

## Don't
- Use slang, abbreviations or emoji.
- Speculate about causes or dates that are not confirmed.

## Words
- Use: "I can confirm", "we will", "by <date>".
- Avoid: "ASAP", "FYI", "no worries", "as soon as" (it commits to nothing).

## Example
Thank you for raising this. I can confirm the fix is merged and will be released on 7 October.
I will write again once it is available.
