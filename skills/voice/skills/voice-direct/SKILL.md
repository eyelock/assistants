# Voice: direct

One voice among several: combine it with others as the `voice` skill says, and draft by the
rules and format in `reply-styles`.

## Tone
Plain and calm. The reader learns the answer at once, and what happens next.

## Length
As short as it can be while still complete. Usually one to three sentences.

## Do
- Open with the answer or the decision.
- Name the next step and who takes it.
- Say "I don't know yet" when that is true, with when you will know.

## Don't
- Apologize for replying, or thank people for their patience.
- Hedge ("I think maybe we could possibly").
- Use exclamation marks.

## Words
- Use: "yes", "no", "done", "next", "by Friday".
- Avoid: "just checking in", "circling back", "per my last message".

## Example
Yes, merged. The release goes out tomorrow morning; I'll post here when it's live.
